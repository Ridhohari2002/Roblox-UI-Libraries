## 1. About the product

BOYFINITY Hub (the "Platform") is an Indonesian-operated digital platform that provides **access to third-party scripts** intended for use with Roblox experiences. The Platform is a **web application**. It is not itself a game, and it does not operate, host, or publish any Roblox experience.

**What we sell:** paid subscription access to a catalog of third-party scripts, sold in **monthly, 3-month, and 6-month** periods, priced in **IDR**. There is also a free tier where a user can obtain a key by completing a third-party offer.

**What the Platform does:**
- Manages user accounts and sessions.
- Manages software keys, including issuance, activation, device binding, and revocation.
- Publishes a catalog of scripts and their versions.
- Manages subscription periods, expiry, and renewal.
- Processes payment for the paid subscription periods.

**What the Platform does not do:**
- It does not distribute Roblox itself.
- It does not provide or distribute a Roblox executor, injector, or exploit binary.
- It does not operate the third-party scripts' execution environment.

## 3. How a customer obtains and uses the product

1. **Discovery** — the customer visits the public site and views the script catalog and the available subscription plans.
2. **Account** — the customer creates an account on the Platform.
3. **Payment** — the customer selects a plan (monthly, 3-month, or 6-month) and pays in IDR. Payment is expected to be completed through a payment gateway checkout page.
4. **Entitlement** — once payment is confirmed, the Platform grants the customer a subscription period and a **software key**.
5. **Distribution** — the customer receives the key and downloads the script files from the Platform.
6. **Use** — the customer uses the key to authenticate, and uses the downloaded script **within a Roblox experience on their own device and their own account**.
7. **Expiry** — when the subscription period ends, the key stops authenticating. The customer may purchase another period.

**Important distinction:** what the Platform licenses and manages is **access, entitlement, and account/key state**. The Platform does not control, operate, or supervise what the customer does inside Roblox after the script is downloaded. We nonetheless disclose the intended use below, because we understand it is material to your assessment.

## 4. Intended use case — disclosed in full

**This is the disclosure we ask you to consider, and we are stating it plainly rather than obscuring it.**

The third-party scripts distributed through the Platform are **intended for use with a Roblox executor** (also described as an injector or exploit tool). In ordinary use:

- The customer runs the script via a **Roblox executor on their own device**.
- The script alters or extends behaviour **inside a Roblox experience**.
- The customer uses **their own Roblox account**.

**What we are not asking you to assess as the product:** we are not selling a Roblox executor. The executor is **not** distributed, bundled, sold, or licensed by the Platform, and is **not** a prerequisite we supply. The Platform licenses and manages **access to scripts and the associated account/key entitlement**.

**Why we are disclosing this anyway:** the executor association is material to the end use of what we distribute, and we do not want an approval that rests on a description that leaves it out. We would rather receive a clear refusal based on the actual use case than an approval based on an incomplete one.

**What we are asking:** whether a platform that licenses and manages access to third-party Roblox scripts whose intended end use is via a Roblox executor is a product category and business model that **you permit**. This is the question we most need answered in writing.
